# Shaksuka

## Ingredients
* Eggs
* Tomatoes
* Bell peppers
* Chilli
* Harissa
* Coriander
* smoked paprika
* stock
* olive oil

## Instructions
1. First cut all vegetables
2. Fry all vegetables for 2 min
3. No add the spices, mix and fry for 2 min
4. Add water, stock
5. Let t sit witht he eggs for 10 minutes

Eat!
